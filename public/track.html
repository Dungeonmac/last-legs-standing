How to use it, in 5 steps

  1. Go outside and wait for the green dot.

    The GPS dot is at the top right of this page.

  2. Tap START RUN.

    Keep this page open and your screen on.

  3. Run until “To go today” shows ✓.

    Then run a little more, in case your GPS reads short.

  4. Tap FINISH & SUBMIT.

    Wait for “Run approved”.

  5. Done for today.

    Check My race for the green ✓.

Before you go

  • Keep this page open and on screen. We keep your screen awake for you while the tracker is open. Don’t press the lock button or switch to another app: phones pause GPS for websites in the background. If you want to listen to music or use your phone during the run, start the music first, or record with Strava or your watch instead.
  • Add this page to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, ⋮ → Install app on Android) to use it like an app.
  • Your track is saved on your phone as you go, so a refresh won’t lose it.
  • The run must start and finish before the 23:59:59 UTC deadline. You’ll get a warning 15 minutes before.

Not sure what to do?

Follow the step-by-step guide, or contact us. Reach out early: help requests don’t extend the deadline.
